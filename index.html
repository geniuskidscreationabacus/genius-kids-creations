Abacus

The IPA Abacus programme

Ages 4 to 14 · Foundation to Grand Master

What it is

Abacus training uses a bead counting frame to teach children fast, accurate mental arithmetic. With regular practice, students learn to picture the abacus in their mind and calculate without needing the physical tool, which is what produces the speed you see in demonstrations.

How the curriculum is structured

Students move through graded levels, from Foundation through Junior, Intermediate and Advanced, up to Grand Master. Each level builds on the last, with an assessment at the end before moving on, so progress is certified rather than assumed.

What it builds

  • Faster, more accurate mental calculation
  • Whole-brain development, using both hands together
  • Longer concentration span, which carries into other subjects
  • Stronger visual memory and recall
Genius Kids Creations follows the IPA (Ideal Play Abacus India) curriculum, which has been running since 2003 across 1,500+ centres in India and abroad.

Vedic Mathematics

The Vedic Mathematics programme

Age 12 and above · Level 1 to 3

What it is

Vedic Mathematics is a set of calculation techniques and shortcuts drawn from traditional Indian methods. Instead of replacing school arithmetic, it gives students faster routes through the same problems, in multiplication, division, squares, and more.

Who it suits

It works best for older students who already have a maths foundation and want to calculate faster under time pressure, particularly ahead of board exams and competitive entrance tests where speed matters as much as accuracy.

How the curriculum is structured

The programme runs across three levels. Level 1 covers core techniques, Level 2 builds on them with more complex operations, and Level 3 applies them to the kind of problems students meet in board and entrance exams.
